WEBSITE DESIGN

Flex Use Commercial Complex Website

THE BRIEF

Vacancies were hitting the market, but the complex looked like every other listing.

Ownership of a 15-unit flex use complex had vacancies coming to market and was having trouble getting commercial agents to differentiate the property from others in the area. Without a dedicated presence of its own, the complex was competing on the same generic listing sheets as everyone else.

HOW WE HELPED

A dedicated website, branding, and signage built around the property itself.

Kora Commercial met with ownership, toured the complex, and built a custom website with branding dedicated to the property's design and the leasing goals of ownership and their representatives. Current vacancies are identified directly on the site. New signage with QR codes was printed on-site to direct interested parties straight to the website, and email campaigns were developed to match the branding.

THE RESULT

A property with its own identity — and a direct path from curiosity to inquiry.

The complex now has a dedicated online presence that gives agents and prospective tenants a clear, branded way to explore available space, rather than relying on generic listings alone. Signage, email, and the website all work together to move interest straight into inquiries.
