SPACE RENDERING

Space Renderings

THE BRIEF

An empty or unfinished space is hard for a prospective tenant to picture as their own.

Raw architectural photos and bare shell spaces ask a lot of imagination from a prospective tenant. Ownership and their agents needed a way to show what a space could become — fully designed, furnished, and in use — before a single dollar was spent on buildout.

HOW WE HELPED

Photorealistic renderings that turn a shell into a finished vision.

Kora Digital produces detailed renderings of a space in its finished, designed state — furniture, fixtures, lighting, and layout all included. Each rendering is built directly from the original space, giving ownership and their agents marketing material that shows exactly what the space can be.

THE RESULT

Listings that sell the vision, not just the square footage.

With finished renderings in hand, agents can market a space with the same polish as a move-in-ready listing, well before construction begins. Prospective tenants get a clear picture of what they're leasing, which helps move spaces from curiosity to signed deal faster.
