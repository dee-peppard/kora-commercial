WEBSITE DESIGN

Broker Agent Marketing Website

THE BRIEF

A broker with real results, but no digital footprint of their own.

Individual brokers close the deals, but their marketing usually lives entirely under their brokerage's banner. Without a personal website, a broker has no independent SEO presence and no single place to point new clients to see the transactions they've closed and the services they offer.

HOW WE HELPED

A personal site built to showcase transactions and services.

Kora Digital builds each broker a dedicated agent website that puts their own name and track record front and center. The site includes a Select Transactions section highlighting top deals — property, size, and role on each — alongside a full list of the broker's advisory services, from tenant representation to buyer representation and landlord leasing & management.

THE RESULT

Independent SEO, and a link the broker actually owns.

With their own site live, brokers build search visibility tied to their own name rather than relying solely on their brokerage. It gives them a professional home to send prospective clients, include in an email signature, and share on social media — entirely their own.
