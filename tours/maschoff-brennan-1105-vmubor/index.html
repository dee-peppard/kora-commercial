The 2nd floor at 137 East 25th offers a full-floor creative canvas ideal for wellness, production, and experiential concepts. With high ceilings, wide-plank wood floors, and clean white brick finishes, the space blends warmth with versatility, perfect for uses such as a Pilates or yoga studio, photo/film production, art gallery, or brand showroom.

Unlike traditional office space, this level offers privacy, controlled lighting, and a calm, boutique feel that supports client-driven experiences. The open floor plan allows flexible buildout options to fit your vision.

Tenants benefit from a newly renovated lobby, a full-time doorman, and dual access from East 25th and East 26th Streets, creating a discreet yet easily accessible entry for staff and visitors.

Located where NoMad meets Gramercy, 137 East 25th is steps from Madison Square Park, Upland, and the 6 and R/W trains, placing your brand in one of Manhattan’s most vibrant, walkable neighborhoods.
