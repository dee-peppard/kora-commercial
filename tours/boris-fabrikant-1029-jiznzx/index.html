From the listing

Located in Murray Hill, on a 14,400 square foot lot, the existing 6 story parking garage consists of about 90,000 square feet above grade. There is a full cellar of approximately 14,000 square feet. The roof is currently used for parking and accessed through 2 freight elevators and would make for an unbelievable rooftop garden. The slab to slab ceiling heights through the building range from about 11’ in the cellar to 14’ on the column free ground floor and 12’ on the upper floors. The property is located in an R8B zoning district. Because of its use as a parking garage, the building can be converted to any use which is less onerous than Use Group 8 which includes use group 6 (Retail), Use Group 3-4 (Community Facility) and of course residential. There is the possibility of putting windows along the rear, East and West portions of the building. The existing live load on each floor is 120 pounds per square foot. This large property is located on a relatively quiet street just south of Murray Hill. It is located just a short distance from the entrance and exits of Queens Midtown Tunnel and from the FDR Drive on 34th Street. Subway lines and depots are within short walking distances. It is proximate to NYU Medical and Alexandria.
